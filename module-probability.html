Tactical Voting

Our default model already factors in a degree of tactical voting within the broad left-of-centre and right-of-centre blocs. Most recent polling suggests this effect will be stronger on the left than the right or, more specifically, when directed against Reform, but whether it fully materialises is difficult to say. Westminster by-elections provide some insight into how effective tactical voting can be in a high stakes / high attention environment that won't necessarily be repeated in a general election. You can use the simple tactical voting calculator to simulate what higher rates of it would look like in practice.
